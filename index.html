Our Story

Who We Are

Born from a Passion
for Good Food

Restaurant Foleja e Shqiponjave was founded in 2023 with a vision to create a dining destination that blends Traditional warmth with the rich culinary traditions of the Balkans. Situated along the scenic road between Tetovë and Bozovcë, we are proud to serve guests from Tetovo and the wider region.

Our name — Foleja e Shqiponjave — evokes the spirit of the Traditional coast: sunlit terraces, Traditional Food, vibrant herbs, and the kind of hospitality that makes every guest feel like family. We brought that spirit to the heart of North Macedonia.

From day one, our goal has been simple: serve exceptional food in a space where people can relax, connect, and make memories. Every detail — from the warm interior to the handcrafted menus — reflects this commitment.

Our Approach

Quality in Every
Detail

We work closely with local producers and trusted suppliers to ensure every ingredient that arrives in our kitchen meets our exacting standards. Freshness is non-negotiable — from the vegetables in our salads to the fish on our grill.

Our team of dedicated chefs brings together classical technique and regional inspiration to create dishes that feel both familiar and exciting. Each plate is an expression of our respect for ingredients and our love of hospitality.

Whether you're joining us for a quick lunch, a family dinner, or a special celebration, we promise the same warmth, care, and quality every time.

Our Values

The Foleja e Shqiponjave Philosophy

Fresh Ingredients

We source seasonal, locally-grown produce and premium ingredients for every dish we serve.

Warm Service

Our team treats every guest with genuine warmth, attention, and the spirit of true Traditional hospitality.

Quality Cuisine

From traditional Balkan dishes to contemporary Traditional plates, quality is our standard — not our goal.

Comfortable Atmosphere

A space designed to feel relaxing, elegant, and welcoming — the perfect setting for any occasion.
